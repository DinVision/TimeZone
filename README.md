# Din Time Zone

An auto-hide clock panel for Windows. Move the mouse to the screen edge and it slides out. Move away and it hides again. For any inquiries on this App, please email me at dinvision.bc@gmail.com ![](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bfe4f8f7-8c61-4549-a91c-ed43d1b278ce)

## Features

- **Multiple clocks**, each digital or analog, with any time zone
- Optional date, day of the week (Sun, Mon...), 12 or 24-hour time, and seconds
- **Countdown timers** with a repeating beep (for example, every 10 minutes). Click a timer to start or pause it.
- **Auto-hide** on any screen edge (top, bottom, left, right). No clicking needed.
- **Pin button** to keep the panel visible for a while
- Drag the panel near an edge to dock it, or drop it anywhere else to leave it floating
- Dark, Light, Follow Windows, or custom theme, plus accent colors and rounded corners
- Works fully offline. Settings are saved in a simple JSON file.

## How to use
- **Right-click** the panel to add clocks and timers, change the size, theme, and auto-hide options, or open About.
- **Click-drag** to move the panel.
- **Ctrl + mouse wheel** makes the panel bigger or smaller.
